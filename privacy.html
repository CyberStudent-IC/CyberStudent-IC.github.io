Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Who uses it

snorlax-youtube is used only by its owner, on his own household YouTube account. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, it reads and changes that account's playlists, the items in them, and its channel subscriptions, only when the owner asks for a change.

Where data goes

Data stays on the owner's own computer and home server. Backups of playlists and subscriptions are kept there for no more than 30 days. Nothing is sold, shared, sent to anyone else, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all access immediately.
